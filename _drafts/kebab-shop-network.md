When I got to the new location, there was absolutely nothing. The owner had stripped the place down to the bare stone.

And he wanted everything: Wi-Fi, cameras, the register, TVs. At the start he even wanted an office spot in the basement. (That never happened.)

## A 500 m spool of Cat6

Walls stripped to the stone is the best possible moment to run cable. So I gave him my 500 m spool of fire-rated Cat6, which I'd picked up at an outlet sale for €135.

The deal: he pulls cable wherever he thinks he'll need it. I helped plan the main runs: down to the basement, to the camera spots, to where the TVs would hang, and into the kitchen.
