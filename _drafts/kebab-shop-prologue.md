> **Note:** this all started about four years ago, long before I used Claude. Claude helps me write it up.

I'm a semi-regular at a local kebab shop. Back at their old location, the owner found out I do IT. You can probably guess what happened next.

The first call was about Thuisbezorgd. Their orders came in through T-Connect, running on an old-as-hell laptop in a corner of the kitchen, next to a printer covered in grease. And that printer was being a pain again.

So I fixed it. Then, because I was honestly just tired of that printer, I replaced it with a small USB receipt printer.

That was close to four years ago. I'm still the person who gets called.

The good news: this owner is the opposite of [the sandwich shop guy](/2026/11/sandwich-shop-cameras/). He listens, and he'd rather do it right the first time, even if it costs a bit more. Which is how a kebab shop ended up with everything in this series.
